# Independent Validation Boundary

Stage 05G's D-R3 validation is independent of the Stage 04B VALIDATION and SEALED_TEST roles. It may occur only after the K=1 and sealed-transfer prerequisites and under a separate prospective lineage, access, metric, and adjudication contract.

D-R3 contributes nothing to `s_a`, `u_a`, `L_def`, Stage 05B/C thresholds, Stage 05E budgets, checkpoint selection, or Stage 05F test release. It may corroborate or challenge a frozen claim, but may not be used as an extra tuning pool or substitute for a failed Stage 04B family.
