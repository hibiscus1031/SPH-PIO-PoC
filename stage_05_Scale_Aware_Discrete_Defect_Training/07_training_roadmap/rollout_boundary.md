# Rollout Boundary

Stage 05A authorizes no rollout. Stage 05E is K=1 training only; Stage 05F evaluates frozen checkpoints under its preregistered one-step transfer protocol and does not silently become autonomous rollout.

Autonomous rollout is conditional Stage 05G work under a new contract. That contract must freeze initial-state access, causal history updates, ephemeral midpoint semantics, graph rebuilds, failure/topology rules, horizons, metrics, uncertainty comparisons, and termination behavior before execution. Rollout diagnostics cannot retroactively select Stage 05E training settings or Stage 05F checkpoints.
