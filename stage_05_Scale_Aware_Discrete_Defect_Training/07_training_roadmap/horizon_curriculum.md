# Horizon Curriculum

The first formal training horizon is uniquely `K=1`. No horizon curriculum, teacher-forced K=2 objective, density/position objective, or autonomous rollout may be activated in Stage 05E merely because K=1 underperforms.

K=2 consideration requires Stage 05B PASS, Stage 05C PASS, completion of Stage 05D, and Stage 05E K=1 plus Stage 05F sealed-transfer PASS under their frozen criteria. Stage 05G must then preregister horizon construction, accepted-history semantics, loss components and weights, budgets, topology rules, checkpoint policy, and failure handling before any K=2 execution.
