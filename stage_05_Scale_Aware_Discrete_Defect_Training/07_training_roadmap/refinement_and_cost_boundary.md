# Refinement and Cost Boundary

Stage 05H conditionally evaluates time-step, spatial-resolution, and support refinement; equal-error cost; and publication readiness. Before execution it must freeze refinement families, error reference, norms, uncertainty floors, equal-error interpolation, hardware/software accounting, warm-up and timing rules, failure censoring, and claim gates.

Refinement and cost results may not change earlier splits, scales, losses, checkpoints, or sealed-test decisions. A full claim of a trained conservative SPH–Transformer hybrid solver requires strong Stage 05G/H evidence. Otherwise the project retains a verification-first/negative-evidence methods-paper route.
