# Validation Access Boundary

During Stages 05A–05C, `validation target decode = 0`; validation state/target payloads may not select targets, conservative-coverage gates, `s_a`, `u_a`, losses, thresholds, coordinates, directions, budgets, or model configurations.

Stage 05D may open VALIDATION states and targets only after the complete training protocol, model-arm contract, budgets, checkpoint rule, qualification gates, and access audit are frozen and hashed. Validation then serves only its preregistered checkpoint and transfer role. Any earlier access is a seal violation and invalidates progression.
