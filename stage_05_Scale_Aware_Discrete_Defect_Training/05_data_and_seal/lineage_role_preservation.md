# Lineage Role Preservation

The formula-level connected lineage is the indivisible split atom. A frame, window, particle, edge, resolution, or variant is never an independent split atom. All descendants of a formula component retain its Stage 04B role.

Stage 05B may materialize targets only for the six TRAIN lineages after its numeric gates and decode plan are preregistered. Validation and sealed-test descendants remain inaccessible. Every later artifact must carry lineage, role, variant, resolution, origin, parent-trajectory identity, and source hashes sufficient to audit zero cross-role leakage.

Observed quality, scale, loss, gradient, or performance may never trigger reassignment or replacement.
