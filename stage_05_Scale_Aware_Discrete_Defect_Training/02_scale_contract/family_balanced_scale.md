# Family-Balanced Scale

Balancing order is part of the scale identity: node mean within origin; equal origin mean within variant; equal variant mean within family; equal mean across the six TRAIN families. Pooling all nodes or origins before averaging is prohibited because it would weight large resolutions or long trajectories more heavily.

The immutable TRAIN set is `LCDF_01`, `LCDF_04`, `LCDF_05`, `LCDF_06`, `LCDF_07`, and `LCDF_08`. Only variants and origins that pass the prospectively frozen Stage 05B eligibility rules may contribute; a failing family may not be removed or replaced to improve the scale. Counts and exclusions must be reported before the numeric value is accepted.
