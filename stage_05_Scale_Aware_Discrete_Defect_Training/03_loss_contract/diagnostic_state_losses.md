# Diagnostic State Losses

During the first formal K=1 route, velocity, position, density, pressure, and energy state residuals may be reported only as diagnostics under a later authorized execution protocol. They carry zero training weight and may not select the scale, checkpoint, optimizer, budget, or arm.

This boundary follows the intervention: the learned reciprocal term directly corrects momentum acceleration; position sensitivity enters at `dt^2` order and density responds indirectly through continuity. Stage 04's original one-step state MSE produced an insufficient parameter-level task signal, but that historical result is not reclassified or claimed repaired here.
