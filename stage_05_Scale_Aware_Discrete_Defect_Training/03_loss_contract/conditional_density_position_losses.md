# Conditional Density and Position Losses

Density, position, and longer-horizon velocity state losses may be considered only after all of the following have passed under their own frozen contracts:

1. Stage 05B target, conservative-compatibility, scale, and uncertainty qualification;
2. Stage 05C gradient and no-writeback local-descent qualification;
3. Stage 05E K=1 training and the Stage 05F frozen-checkpoint transfer assessment.

Any K=2 velocity, density, position, or autonomous-rollout objective requires a new prospective contract before evaluation. Such terms may not be introduced reactively after first-round failure, and Stage 05A does not set their weights or thresholds.
