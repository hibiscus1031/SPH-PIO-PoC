# Initialization and Fairness Contract

Every D1/D2/D3 run starts from a fresh, prospectively specified initialization and reads no Stage 02, Stage 03, or Stage 04 weights or checkpoints. Initialization schemes, seed lists, seed derivation, parameter dtype, common-head treatment, and parameter hashes must be frozen before Stage 05C execution.

Fair comparison requires the same legal tokens, reciprocal head, qualified TRAIN origins, single `s_a`, canonical `L_def`, optimizer family, budget principle, checkpoint rule, and validation/test protocol. Architectural parameter counts and realized compute are reported rather than falsely equalized. No arm-specific scale, loss, favorable family, post hoc seed, or expanded budget is permitted.
