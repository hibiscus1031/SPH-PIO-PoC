# Stage 05A Scope

## Purpose

Freeze a scale-aware, baseline-centered discrete-defect training hypothesis and a staged qualification contract while retaining the complete RK2 solver, reciprocal antisymmetric pair-force architecture, and Stage 04B sealed family protocol.

## In scope

- prospective definitions of `S_0^(n+1)`, `S_theta^(n+1)`, `Delta_v_def^star`, `Delta_v_eff^theta`, `a_def^star`, `a_eff^theta`, `a_cm^star`, `a_cons^star`, and `a_incompatible^star`;
- the TRAIN-only scalar scale formula `s_a` and uncertainty scale `u_a` interface;
- the unique primary loss and diagnostic-only state quantities;
- four-layer Stage 05C gradient qualification;
- immutable 6/2/2 lineage roles and decode boundaries;
- fair D1/D2/D3 arms and the Stage 05B–05H roadmap.

## Out of scope and prohibited

Target or trajectory materialization, D0 defect computation, payload decode, model instantiation/forward, derivative probes, optimizer construction, training, parameter writeback, checkpoint creation, validation decode, sealed-test decode, rollout, and performance evaluation are all prohibited. Stage 04 thresholds, verdicts, counts, splits, and historical artifacts may not be modified or reinterpreted.

## Completion rule

Stage 05A completes only when all required contract files and manifests exist, every identity and boundary is unique, all historical states remain frozen, and every prohibited execution count is zero.
