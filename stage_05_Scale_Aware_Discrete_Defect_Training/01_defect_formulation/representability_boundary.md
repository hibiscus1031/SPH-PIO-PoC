# Representability Boundary

The common pair head produces reciprocal antisymmetric pair forces. Consequently the learned correction has exactly zero mass-weighted total force by construction and can represent only the zero-center subspace containing `a_cons^star`.

The spatially uniform `a_incompatible^star = a_cm^star` lies outside that subspace. The model must never be required to fit it, and no conservation, antisymmetry, or center-of-mass projection penalty may be added to compensate for a target-contract error.

Before any TRAIN target decode, Stage 05B must preregister a numeric conservative-coverage gate and its uncertainty comparison. If incompatible content exceeds that frozen gate, the required verdict is `Stage 05B NOT_QUALIFIED`; changing the target, family pool, or gate after observing results is prohibited.
