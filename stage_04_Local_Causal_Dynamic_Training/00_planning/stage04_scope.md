# Stage 04A scope

## Scientific scope

Stage 04 is a new research route. Its hypothesis is that conservative dynamic neural-SPH models can receive qualifiable training signals from task-aligned local-causal RK2 state transitions, initially at `K=1` and conditionally at short window `K=2`, without requiring the unqualified full `K=8` reverse-mode chain from Stage 03D.

This route is not Stage 03E, not Stage 03D gradient contract v0.2, not a repair or override of the Stage 03D verdict, not a reinterpretation obtained by changing attention backend, and not Stage 02 static protocol v0.3. All Stage 04 model arms are newly initialized and use new prospectively qualified dynamic data.

## Stage 04A deliverables

Stage 04A freezes:

- the unique `K=1`, `H=4` local-causal training transition;
- the accepted-state loss family and prohibited targets/penalties;
- the optimizer-variable-only hard gradient boundary;
- CPU float64 and fixed PyTorch math SDPA for formal D3 evidence;
- prospective data-family, lineage, split, leakage, and sealed-test requirements;
- fair D1/D2/D3 comparison invariants;
- success-gate categories, horizon progression, and Stage 04B–04G dependencies.

Stage 04A deliberately does not freeze numerical component weights, normalization constants, optimizer hyperparameters, resource ceilings, checkpoint details, or numerical pass thresholds. Those quantities must be prospectively registered in Stage 04D after Stage 04B data qualification and Stage 04C gradient qualification, but before Stage 04E results exist.

## Prohibited execution

Stage 04A performs no trajectory generation, test creation or release, target/state decoding, optimizer construction or stepping, parameter update, training, rollout, model comparison, performance evaluation, backend comparison, new gradient probe, new epsilon study, or modification of Stage 01–03 artifacts. It makes no trainability or performance claim.

## Completion and authorization

Completion requires every Stage 04A contract and manifest to be present and internally consistent, all historical status boundaries to be preserved, and the Stage 03D-S frozen 1976-file set to reverify with zero missing and zero mismatched hashes. On completion, the sole next-stage authorization is Stage 04B. No training is authorized.
