# Stage 04B–04G roadmap

## Stage 04B — New Dynamic Reference-Family Pool and Lineage Qualification

Prospectively define, generate, audit, and qualify new D-R1 formula families covering compression, shear–compression coupling, rotating/deforming, multimode, and controlled low-Mach regimes. Freeze complete-lineage components and disjoint train/validation/sealed roles; keep D-R3 independent only. No training.

## Stage 04C — Task-Aligned Parameter-Gradient Qualification

Using the exact K=1 `L_state`, qualify optimizer-parameter groups with reverse VJP, forward JVP, central FD at at least three epsilons, stable-window rules, and deterministic repeats. Formal D3 evidence is CPU float64 math SDPA only. No training.

## Stage 04D — Training Protocol Preregistration and Sealed-Test Preflight

Freeze numerical loss weights/normalization, optimizer/budget, initialization, checkpoint selection, success thresholds, uncertainty/aggregation, test access controls, run schema, and failure policy. Demonstrate target/state decode count `0`. No formal training before completion.

## Stage 04E — Formal K=1 Training of D1/D2/D3

Freshly initialize all arms and train with the same legal tokens, common pair head, split, loss, optimizer budget, and checkpoint rule. Freeze checkpoints before sealed-test release and report all preregistered failures. Do not make an autonomous-solver claim.

## Stage 04F — Conditional K=2 Training and Autonomous Rollout

Only after successful K=1 qualification, training, and sealed evaluation, prospectively qualify and optionally train K=2. Then evaluate autonomous rollout under separate preregistered stability and conservation gates. K=4/K=8 are not the first-round backpropagation horizon.

## Stage 04G — Independent D-R3 Validation, Refinement and Utility Assessment

Apply frozen models/protocols to D-R3 oblique shear as independent validation only, then assess resolution/time-step refinement and scientific utility. Only the combined autonomous, independent, and refinement evidence can support a bounded full-solver claim.

## Non-skipping rule

04B, 04C, and 04D are mandatory predecessors of 04E. Authorization is stage-local; a prior completion does not silently authorize later execution.
