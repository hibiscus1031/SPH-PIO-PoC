# Short-window progression

The mandatory order is:

1. K=1 task-aligned parameter-gradient qualification;
2. K=1 formal training;
3. K=1 sealed evaluation;
4. optional K=2 gradient qualification;
5. optional K=2 training;
6. autonomous rollout evaluation.

K=2 is not automatically authorized by a passing K=1 gradient check or training fit. Its transition composition, teacher-forcing boundary, parameter-gradient probes, thresholds, budget, and checkpoint rule require a prospective Stage 04F contract. K=2 may proceed only after the preregistered K=1 qualification, training, and sealed-evaluation requirements have been satisfied.

`K=4` and `K=8` are excluded from the first Stage 04 training backpropagation horizon. Stage 04 does not reuse the Stage 03D K=8 long-chain route because that route remains not qualified and its failure attribution remains mixed or unresolved. This exclusion is a change of scientific hypothesis and task alignment, not evidence that the former gradients were repaired.

Short-window success has a narrow meaning: it can support learnability of the preregistered local transition under the tested data and environment. It cannot establish autonomous long-horizon stability, topology robustness, refinement, independent-family transfer, or complete solver validity. Those claims require later Stage 04F/04G evidence.
