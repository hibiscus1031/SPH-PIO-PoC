# New trajectory-family requirements

Stage 04B must prospectively generate and qualify a new D-R1 dynamic trajectory-family pool. The 18 existing Stage 03B trajectories may remain historical/reference evidence but cannot be re-divided into Stage 04 train, validation, or sealed-test sets.

The new pool must contain multiple distinct analytic material-map formula lineages and collectively cover at least:

- compression;
- shear–compression coupling;
- a rotating/deforming mode;
- multimode deformation;
- different but controlled low-Mach regimes.

Stage 04B must freeze governing formulae, admissible parameter ranges, reference construction, numerical tolerances, state schema, origin eligibility, uncertainty treatment, and family qualification criteria before payload generation or qualification outcomes are interpreted.

Changing `N`, `dt`, phase, amplitude, or support for one analytic formula does not by itself create an independent lineage. Such variants remain within the same formula-lineage component unless Stage 04B provides a strict, prospective scientific basis for a different component assignment. This prevents near-duplicate variants from leaking across roles.

No trajectory is generated in Stage 04A. New data acquire a downstream role only after Stage 04B qualification; failed and excluded families remain recorded rather than silently removed.
