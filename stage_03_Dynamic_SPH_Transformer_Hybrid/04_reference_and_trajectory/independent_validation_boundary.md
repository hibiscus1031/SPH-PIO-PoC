# Independent-validation boundary

D-R3 and D-R4 families are isolated from training, normalization, architecture choice, horizon choice, loss weights, thresholds, checkpoint selection, early stopping, and debugging feedback. Their lineage components must not connect to development data.

Stage 01 R3 shear/acoustic evidence remains historical independent-validation evidence and does not become Stage 03 training data or a fresh blind test. Stage 03 must create or qualify new training, family-isolated validation, sealed-test, and independent source-free validation families. Reusing a formula with changed resolution, `dt`, or start time does not establish independence.

Access to sealed or independent outcomes consumes that role for subsequent adaptive decisions and must be logged. D-R4 is currently `NOT_AVAILABLE`; higher-resolution SPH may be D-R2 or an uncertainty study but cannot be promoted to D-R4 without independent V&V qualification.
