# Figure contract — CMAME frozen evidence suite v1

## Global contract

- **Core conclusion:** A conservative neural-SPH correction route must pass distinct
  structure, target, gradient, optimizer, training, validation, support, and governance
  qualifications; in this frozen project several lower layers qualified, while both
  formal campaigns and final target-manifold coverage did not establish a trained
  solver.
- **Archetype:** asymmetric mixed-modality suite, with schematic-led method figures and
  quantitative evidence grids.
- **Target/output:** CMAME/Elsevier, 190 mm full width; editable SVG, font-embedded PDF,
  and 600 dpi PNG; Python/matplotlib only.
- **Evidence hierarchy:** frozen machine-readable results and histories are primary;
  frozen qualification summaries and closure ledgers are validation/interpretive
  boundaries; conceptual schematics are explanatory only and trace to frozen contracts.
- **Statistics:** no new inferential test, fit, threshold, confidence interval, or
  scientific aggregate is introduced.  Seeds and failed runs are shown individually.
  Histograms, sorting, binning, and coordinate placement are deterministic display
  transformations.
- **Source data:** every quantitative panel exports the exact plotted frozen values to a
  local source-data JSON/CSV and records the original source hash and field.
- **Image integrity:** no raster screenshot, selective point removal, local contrast
  adjustment, or manual data editing.  Schematic geometry is native vector drawing.
- **Reviewer risk:** the visual grammar must not imply solver success, Transformer
  superiority, general neural-SPH failure, full V2 qualification, target truth,
  all-coordinate FD qualification, autonomous rollout, or sealed-test performance.

## Graphical abstract

- **Core conclusion:** qualification gates progressively separate verified/qualified
  components from the unqualified full-solver route.
- **Panel map:** a single left-to-right evidence route from verified SPH foundations to
  evidence-based closure, with status-coded branches.
- **Hero evidence:** frozen cross-stage progression and final route-closure flags.
- **Risk:** it must look like route closure, not a successful solver pipeline.
