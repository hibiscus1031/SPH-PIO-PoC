# Stage08 scope

Stage08A tests H08-01 only: prospective selection under frozen formula,
physics, raw-defect, and oracle-coefficient coverage constraints may avoid the
held-out support gap created by Stage07's hash-based within-stratum role split.
It is a pool-design and qualification stage, not a training stage.

Formal TRAIN_V3 is fixed at 14 lineages: six historical anchors and eight
systematically selected new lineages. Fresh validation v3 is fixed at four
independent lineages selected only from a disjoint validation bank.
