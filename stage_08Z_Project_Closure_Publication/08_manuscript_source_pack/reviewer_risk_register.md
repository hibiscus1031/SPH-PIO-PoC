# Reviewer-risk register

## R1

- Likely criticism: Why publish an unqualified trained solver?
- Evidence-based response: The paper is about qualification methodology; complete negative campaigns reveal non-substitutable gates.
- Remaining limitation: External utility must be demonstrated through clear transferable protocols, not solver performance.

## R2

- Likely criticism: Are thresholds arbitrary?
- Evidence-based response: Thresholds were frozen before outcomes and applied without relaxation.
- Remaining limitation: Their external calibration remains problem-dependent.

## R3

- Likely criticism: Is this just extensive debugging?
- Evidence-based response: The stages form a falsifiable causal ladder with immutable verdicts, controlled roles and scientific consequences.
- Remaining limitation: The manuscript must compress engineering detail and foreground general methodology.

## R4

- Likely criticism: Why no sealed-test result?
- Evidence-based response: Release required a qualified checkpoint and validation closure; neither occurred. Opening would be post hoc.
- Remaining limitation: No final predictive generalization estimate is available.

## R5

- Likely criticism: Why retain failed branches?
- Evidence-based response: Failures define boundaries and prevent survivor-biased claims.
- Remaining limitation: Readers may question space; use the failure graph and supplement.

## R6

- Likely criticism: Why is high-resolution SPH not truth?
- Evidence-based response: Stage01 separated model-form and finite-resolution effects; resolution alone did not qualify truth.
- Remaining limitation: An independent higher-fidelity reference is still absent.

## R7

- Likely criticism: Does antisymmetry guarantee all conservation?
- Evidence-based response: It supports reciprocal linear-momentum-compatible correction forces in the audited contract only.
- Remaining limitation: It does not guarantee energy, angular momentum, stability or accuracy.

## R8

- Likely criticism: Why compare Transformer if superiority was not tested?
- Evidence-based response: D1–D3 were controlled architecture arms for qualification, not a superiority claim.
- Remaining limitation: No ranking claim can be made.

## R9

- Likely criticism: Is Stage08 support design circular?
- Evidence-based response: Descriptors and normalization were frozen from consumed development evidence before new candidates; model predictions were not read.
- Remaining limitation: The design still used historically consumed cases and therefore is development evidence, not independent confirmation.

## R10

- Likely criticism: Did repeated cycles overfit the research design?
- Evidence-based response: Roles were consumed, new validation was required, verdicts were immutable, and Stage08 was declared final in advance.
- Remaining limitation: The number of development decisions limits external generality and motivates route closure.
